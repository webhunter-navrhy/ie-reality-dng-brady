About the property

Description property

DNG Brady are delighted to bring to the market this charming 2-Bedroom End-of-Terrace Bungalow.

A wonderful opportunity to acquire a charming 2-bedroom end-of-terrace bungalow, ideally situated in a peaceful and quaint cul-de-sac in Drogheda.

The property extends to 63 sq. m. and offers comfortable living in a quiet residential setting, while being conveniently located with Drogheda town centre, shops, restaurants, services, transport links and the local hospital all within easy reach.

The accommodation comprises entrance hall, kitchen, living-room, two bedrooms, bathroom and a sun room.

One of the property's standout features is the large, enclosed and very private rear garden. The garden is not overlooked, creating a wonderfully secluded outdoor space. There is a storage shed which provides useful additional storage.

Early viewing is highly recommended.
